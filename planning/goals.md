# Dotkei Goals and Explicit Non-goals

## Goals
1. Ergonomics

	> Ease-of-use is the most important design goal. This includes strong typing and multi-paradigm concepts.

2. Flexibility

	> Dotkei is not a DSL and should be adaptable to a variety of fields. That said, there are specific fields that dotkei will not be suited for.

3. Performance

	> Fast execution *is* a goal, but it is not the most important.
